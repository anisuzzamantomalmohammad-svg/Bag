Bag

Money in, money out, who owes who

Bag is a free Android app for keeping your own money records. It's built for one person: what you spend, what comes in, the bills you owe and the money people owe you, in any currency.

  • Expenses, income, transfers, I owe and Owed to me in one list
  • Every currency, with a second currency shown under your balance
  • Budgets, savings goals, net worth and a bill calendar
  • Repeats for rent, salary and bills, with reminders before they're due
  • Weekly, monthly and cumulative reports
  • Excel export and import, only when you ask for a file
  • Receipt photos, a home screen widget, app lock and dark mode

Bag has no ads and no account of its own. Your records stay on your phone. If you choose to sign in with Google, sync and backups go to a hidden Bag folder in your own Google Drive.
