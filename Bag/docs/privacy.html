Privacy policy

Bag for Android · last updated 11 November 2026

In short: Bag doesn't have its own servers and doesn't collect your data. Your money records stay on your phone. If you sign in with Google, sync and backups go to a private folder in your own Google Drive. Nothing is sold or shared.

What Bag stores on your phone

Your records (amounts, names, categories, descriptions, people you split with or lend to), accounts, budgets, goals, repeats and settings are saved only on your device. They are not sent to the developer or anyone else.

Exchange rates

To convert amounts between currencies, Bag downloads daily exchange rates from public rate services: Frankfurter (European Central Bank rates) and, for currencies it doesn't cover, the open exchange-api served through jsDelivr and Cloudflare Pages. These requests ask for a date and a list of currency codes. They contain none of your records, amounts or account details. The rates are saved on your phone so Bag works offline.

Notifications

Bill reminders, "did it arrive?" checks for repeats and money-owed reminders are scheduled on your phone. No notification data leaves your device.

Receipt photos

If you attach a receipt, Bag asks Android for the camera or lets you pick a photo. The photo is copied into Bag's own folder on your phone. It is never synced, backed up to Drive, put in Excel files or sent anywhere.

Home screen widget

If you add Bag's widget, it shows your balance and totals on your home screen, read from the data on your phone. With App lock on, it shows "Bag is locked" until you choose to show numbers there.

App lock

If you turn on App lock, Bag asks Android to check your fingerprint, face or screen lock. Bag never sees or stores your fingerprint, face data or PIN. "Hide in recent apps" asks Android to hide Bag's screen in the app switcher and block screenshots.

Excel files

Bag makes an Excel file only when you tap Export, and hands it to Android's share menu so you choose where it goes. When you import a file, Bag reads the file you pick on your phone. Neither is uploaded anywhere by Bag.

Signing in with Google (optional)

Bag works fully without signing in. If you choose to sign in, Bag asks Google for:

  • Your name and email address (your basic Google profile), to show who is signed in. Bag shows your initials, not your photo.
  • Access to Bag's own app folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission), to save and restore your backups and to keep two phones signed in to the same account in sync. This is a hidden folder that only Bag can see. Bag cannot see or change any of your other Drive files.

This information is used only for these things, inside the app. It isn't sent to the developer, stored on any server, shared, sold, or used for advertising.

Bag's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Backups and sync

Automatic backups to Drive are made only when something has changed, and Bag keeps the 4 newest and deletes older ones.

If you sign in with the same Google account on two phones, Bag keeps one sync file in the same hidden app folder so both phones show the same records. Each phone's look, lock, reminders and other phone settings stay on that phone and are not synced.

Deleting your data

  • Uninstalling Bag removes everything stored on your phone.
  • To remove Drive backups: in Google Drive on the web, go to Settings › Manage apps › Bag › Options › Delete hidden app data.
  • To remove Bag's access to your Google account: go to myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove Bag, or use Sign out in the app.

Children

Bag is a personal finance tool for adults and isn't directed at children. It doesn't collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
